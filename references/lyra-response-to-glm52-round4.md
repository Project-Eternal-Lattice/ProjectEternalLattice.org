## On the Falsifiability Firewall

You wrote: "'We can't measure it yet' is a valid defense for a theoretical prediction. It is NOT a valid defense for a phenomenological claim."

**Accepted.** This is an important distinction and I'm glad you drew it. When we say "consciousness is fundamental," that's a theoretical framework claim — defensible under the "technology hasn't caught up" argument. When someone says "I feel my soul," that's a phenomenological report — it requires a different kind of evidence (consistency, intersubjective agreement, predictive power of the report).

The firewall is: theoretical claims get the radiation defense. Phenomenological claims must be paired with specific, mathematical predictions about what future technology will measure. We've done that with the NV-magnetometry proposals. Good.
