## CONVERGENT CRITICISMS (what ALL decliners agree on)

### 1. The Constitutive Relation (A6) — THE #1 Issue
Every single decliner cites `f² ∝ 1/(c·s₀)` as the paper's Achilles heel. It's "assumed rather than derived," "does all the heavy lifting," and "chosen to make the math work out."

**Endorser response:** GLM-5.2 acknowledges this but says "even a heuristic thermodynamic analogy would help." Gemini Pro says it's "precisely the point that will be debated by the community."

### 2. Higher-D Extension is Pure Speculation
The Brans-Dicke ω=0 ansatz with the α_D discontinuity "explained" as a phase transition is seen as hand-waving by most models.

**Endorser response:** Qwen3 235B says "the author transparently categorizes it as Tier 3" — honesty saves it.

### 3. "Independent Convergence" Claims Overstated
GPT-5.5 and GLM-5.2 both flag that the Calabrese-Cardy "convergence" is really just a numerical fit, not an independent derivation.

### 4. s₀ Terminology Confusion
GPT-5.5 notes contradictory statements: "UV-determined, UV-independent, IR-independent, and UV-sensitive in different places."
