# 5. Empirical Methodology and Falsification

The most devastating critique of consciousness-as-fundamental is not internal inconsistency but that it may not rise to the level of being empirically assessable. A theory that cannot be tested is bypassed, not defeated, by evidence. This chapter examines five methodological objections — from Popperian demarcation to measurement circularity to historical analogy — that frame consciousness-as-fundamental as a programme that is not currently a scientific theory, structurally blocked from operationalization, and repeating a historically discredited pattern.

---

## 5.1 Popperian Demarcation Failure

### 5.1.1 Popper's Criterion: A Theory Is Scientific iff It Generates Risky, Testable Predictions with Potential Falsifiers

Popper's demarcation criterion holds that a theory is scientific iff it generates "potential falsifiers," observable states of affairs that would refute it (Popper, 1935/1959, Ch. 4; Popper, 1963, p. 37). Einstein's relativity was exemplary: precise, risky predictions — e.g., light bending near the sun — that could have been disconfirmed (Popper, 1974, pp. 980–981). A theory that "rules out nothing, permits everything" is not scientific (Popper, 1935/1959, pp. 78–92).

### 5.1.2 Consciousness-as-Fundamental Generates No Potential Falsifiers

**(a) STEELMAN STATEMENT.** The claim that "consciousness is the fundamental substrate of reality" fails Popper's criterion. It generates no potential falsifiers — no observable state of affairs is ruled out. A universe with consciousness fundamental looks identical to a materialist universe: the same laws, particles, and causal history (Tononi & Koch, 2015). The conscious aspect is defined as intrinsically private — precisely the feature that makes it allegedly "fundamental" also makes it immune to empirical test. The thesis is compatible with all possible observations, making it a "metaphysical research programme" by Popper's lights (Popper, 1974a, pp. 133–143): heuristically valuable but methodologically second-class (Popper, 1982b, p. 165).

**(b) PRIMARY SOURCE(S).** Popper, *The Logic of Scientific Discovery* (1935/1959), Ch. 4; Popper, *Conjectures and Refutations* (1963), p. 37; Popper, "Intellectual Autobiography" (1974a), pp. 133–143; Popper, *Quantum Theory and the Schism in Physics* (1982b), p. 165; Tononi & Koch (2015), *Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B*, 370(1668).

**(c) SEVERITY.** Show-stopper. If Popper's demarcation criterion is accepted, consciousness-as-fundamental is not a scientific theory but a metaphysical claim, placing it outside empirical adjudication.

**(d) WHAT WOULD ADDRESS IT.** Generate a specific, quantitative prediction following from consciousness-being-fundamental but not materialism — e.g., a measurable deviation from quantum probabilities at the mesoscale, calculable in advance (not post-hoc).

**(e) WHAT WOULD NOT.** Claiming the framework is "beyond" falsification; appealing to confirmation rather than risky prediction; saying materialism is also unfalsifiable; offering post-hoc accommodations as predictions.

### 5.1.3 A "Metaphysical Research Programme" — Heuristically Valuable but Methodologically Second-Class

Popper held such programmes "indispensable for science" (Popper, 1982b, p. 165) as heuristic scaffolding. But the programme itself is not science — a manuscript presenting consciousness-as-fundamental as a *scientific* Theory of Everything commits a category error. Popper's criterion is contested — Kuhn and Feyerabend argued it is too strict (Kuhn, 1962; Feyerabend, 1975) — but even on permissive accounts, a theory must demonstrate some empirical traction, which consciousness-as-fundamental has yet to provide.

## 5.2 Lakatosian Degenerating Research Programme

### 5.2.1 Lakatos's Framework: Programmes Are Progressive if They Generate Novel Confirmed Predictions

Lakatos evaluated "research programmes" — sequences of theories sharing a "hard core" of non-negotiable assumptions, surrounded by a "protective belt" of auxiliary hypotheses modified to absorb anomalies (Lakatos, 1970, pp. 33–34). A programme is *progressive* if new theories have excess empirical content that is corroborated; *degenerating* if modifications are purely defensive (Lakatos, 1970, p. 34; Chalmers, 1994, p. 84).

### 5.2.2 Zero Corroborated Novel Predictions; All Theoretical Development Is Defensive

**(a) STEELMAN STATEMENT.** Consciousness-as-fundamental exhibits hallmarks of degeneration. Its hard core — consciousness is fundamental and ubiquitous, present even in elementary particles (Goff, 2019, pp. 18–20) — has generated no novel, confirmed empirical predictions. Theoretical resources have been spent on defensive manoeuvres: the combination problem emerged as a crisis requiring accommodation, not predicted (Chalmers, 2016, pp. 181–186; Seager, 1995); the unfolding argument showed panpsychist-friendly theories like IIT are pre-falsified (Doerig et al., 2019; Kleiner & Hoel, 2021); no phenomenon has been discovered *guided by* the hypothesis (Tononi & Koch, 2015). The programme's development has occurred "in response to external criticism" rather than "due to its own internal success" (Larvor, 2006, p. 713).

**(b) PRIMARY SOURCE(S).** Lakatos (1970), pp. 33–34; Chalmers (1994), p. 84; Chalmers (2016), pp. 181–186; Seager (1995), *JCS*, 2(3), 272–288; Doerig et al. (2019), *Consciousness and Cognition*, 72, 49–59; Kleiner & Hoel (2021), *Neuroscience of Consciousness*, 2021(1), niab001; Goff (2019), pp. 18–20.

**(c) SEVERITY.** Major. Lakatos does not banish degenerating programmes from rational discourse — he allowed that they may recover — but classifies them as scientifically suspect and intellectually risky.

**(d) WHAT WOULD ADDRESS IT.** Produce at least one novel prediction entailed by the hard core that was not already known, is specific enough to risk disconfirmation, and has been corroborated — e.g., predicting in advance quantum measurement statistics differing between conscious and non-conscious devices, then observing that difference.

**(e) WHAT WOULD NOT.** Listing post-hoc explanations as novel predictions; claiming philosophical sophistication counts as "theoretical progress" — Lakatos required excess *empirical* content; asserting the programme is "young" — Lakatos evaluated on current track record.

### 5.2.3 The Templeton Collaboration's Inconclusive Result Represents the Best Opportunity for Empirical Success — and It Failed to Clearly Support the Thesis

The $6 million Templeton-funded adversarial collaboration between IIT and Global Neuronal Workspace Theory produced a 2025 *Nature* paper reporting both theories were "substantially challenged," with neither achieving clear corroboration (Cogitate Consortium, 2025). For a programme whose credibility rests heavily on IIT, its best empirical opportunity produced not novel confirmed predictions but a muddle — precisely what Lakatos would expect.

## 5.3 Unfalsifiability In Principle

### 5.3.1 Distinguishing "Not Yet Operationalized" from "Unfalsifiable in Principle"

There is a principled distinction between theories "not yet operationalized" (testable once techniques improve) and theories "unfalsifiable in principle" (no possible observation could discriminate them from rivals). General relativity was "not yet operationalized" in 1915; consciousness-as-fundamental is unfalsifiable in principle — and not by accident.

### 5.3.2 The Conscious Aspect Is Defined as Intrinsically Private — Permanently Beyond Empirical Reach by Definitional Fiat

**(a) STEELMAN STATEMENT.** Consciousness-as-fundamental is unfalsifiable in principle because it is observationally equivalent to materialism. If consciousness is ubiquitous — present even in electrons — but does not alter publicly observable particle behavior (if it did, physics experiments would have detected anomalies), then physical laws are identical whether or not consciousness is present (Chalmers, 1996, p. 305). Since all scientific measurement is of physical behavior, there is no empirical access to the conscious aspect. A panpsychist universe and a materialist universe are observationally indistinguishable (Tononi & Koch, 2015). This is structural: the conscious aspect is defined as private and non-physical — not affecting third-person measurable behavior. As Chalmers notes, "there is no direct evidence" for panpsychism, nor "direct evidence against it" — the claim is positioned to evade empirical contact (Chalmers, 1996, p. 305).

**(b) PRIMARY SOURCE(S).** Chalmers (1996), *The Conscious Mind*, p. 305; Tononi & Koch (2015), *Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B*, 370(1668); Popper (1963), p. 37.

**(c) SEVERITY.** Show-stopper. If the thesis is unfalsifiable in principle, it is not an empirical claim at all and cannot be evaluated by scientific methodology.

**(d) WHAT WOULD ADDRESS IT.** Identify a necessary observable consequence of consciousness-being-fundamental that materialism would not predict — e.g., demonstrating that systems with identical physical structure but different conscious states (biological brain vs. functionally identical silicon simulation) *must* exhibit measurably different physical behavior, with the difference specified in advance.

**(e) WHAT WOULD NOT.** Claiming future technology will enable testing (concedes unfalsifiability without addressing the barrier); appealing to first-person experience as "evidence" (begs the question); arguing materialism is also unfalsifiable (tu quoque); pointing to IIT as evidence (pre-falsified by the unfolding argument; Doerig et al., 2019).

### 5.3.3 Searle: The Claim "Does Not Get Up to the Level of Being False"

Searle's critique: panpsychism "does not get up to the level of being false. It is strictly speaking meaningless because no clear notion has been given to the claim" (Searle, cited in "Panpsychism," *Wikipedia*). The problem is not that the claim is false — it fails to meet the minimum conditions for assessability. As Popper observed, "irrefutability is not a virtue of a theory but a vice" (Popper, 1963, p. 37). Defining the central posit as intrinsically private places it beyond the reach of the methodology needed to claim scientific status.

## 5.4 Measurement Circularity (Unfolding Argument and Kleiner-Hoel Dilemma)

### 5.4.1 Doerig et al.'s Hard Criteria: A Theory Must Generate Falsifiable Predictions That Could Be Empirically Disconfirmed

Doerig, Schurger, and Herzog (2020) propose hard criteria for empirical theories of consciousness. But a deeper problem emerges: whether the theory's measurement procedure is independent of what it seeks to explain.

### 5.4.2 Kleiner and Hoel (2021): Any Non-Trivial Theory Relying on Physical Properties Beyond Input-Output Function Is Subject to A Priori Falsification

**(a) STEELMAN STATEMENT.** The "unfolding argument" (Doerig et al., 2019) and its formalization by Kleiner and Hoel (2021) reveal a structural problem: for any theory assigning consciousness based on internal causal structure, that structure can be "unfolded" into a functionally equivalent feedforward system with the same input-output behavior but zero consciousness, by the theory's own criteria. Since the systems are behaviorally indistinguishable, inference based on behavior cannot discriminate between them — yet the theory predicts different conscious states. This systematic mismatch pre-falsifies the theory (Kleiner & Hoel, 2021). Kleiner (2020, 2024) extended this to a no-go theorem: any non-trivial theory relying on properties beyond input-output function is subject to a priori falsification. The Kleiner-Hoel dilemma has two horns: either predictions vary under function-preserving substitutions (a priori false), or they do not (trivial, since predictions are determined by inference data) (Kleiner, 2024; Negro, 2024). Consciousness-as-fundamental faces this acutely: if consciousness is independent of physical structure (as "fundamental" implies), there is no way to measure it independently of the correlates it is supposed to transcend. Measurement becomes circular.

**(b) PRIMARY SOURCE(S).** Doerig et al. (2020), *Cognitive Neuroscience*, 11(3), 1–22; Doerig et al. (2019), *Consciousness and Cognition*, 72, 49–59; Kleiner & Hoel (2021), *Neuroscience of Consciousness*, 2021(1), niab001; Kleiner (2020), *Consciousness and Cognition*, 85, 102981; Kleiner (2024), *Neuroscience of Consciousness*, 2024(1), niae037; Negro (2024), *Neuroscience of Consciousness*, 2024(1), niae012.

**(c) SEVERITY.** Major. The circularity does not refute consciousness-as-fundamental philosophically, but it blocks the path from philosophical claim to empirical science. If the thesis cannot be operationalized without circularity, it cannot ground a scientific research programme.

**(d) WHAT WOULD ADDRESS IT.** Develop an independent measurement procedure not relying on behavioral or neural correlates — a "consciousness detector" measuring the property directly. Alternatively: reformulate to make predictions solely about physical observables sensitive to consciousness's presence, with a principled argument for why those observables are trustworthy indicators.

**(e) WHAT WOULD NOT.** Claiming introspection is the measurement (the correlate needing validation); appealing to IIT's Phi measure (pre-falsified by unfolding); arguing no theory can escape the dilemma (skepticism about all consciousness science, not a defense); saying consciousness is "directly known" (concedes circularity).

### 5.4.3 The Dilemma: Either Predictions Vary Under Function-Preserving Substitutions (A Priori False) or They Don't (Theory Is Trivial)

Either predictions track something beyond observable physical behavior — in which case unfolding-type substitutions falsify the theory a priori — or they do not — in which case the theory adds nothing to the physical description it was supposed to supplement. Consciousness-as-fundamental, asserting consciousness is *more* than physical behavior, is committed to the first horn.

## 5.5 The Vitalism/Aether Historical Analogy

### 5.5.1 Pattern: "Fundamental Unobservable" Posits Are Abandoned When Rivals Explain the Same Phenomena Without Them

The pattern is explanatory superfluity: theories positing a fundamental, unobservable entity tend to be abandoned when a rival explains the same phenomena without the extra posit.

### 5.5.2 Vitalism (Élan Vital) Discredited by Wohler's Urea Synthesis and Molecular Biology; Aether by Maxwell's Equations

Vitalism posited an *elan vital* — a non-physical life force (Bergson, 1911; Driesch, 1914). Luminiferous aether posited a universal medium for light. Both were defended as metaphysically necessary; both discarded because rendered superfluous by rivals: molecular biology for vitalism (Wohler's 1828 urea synthesis showed organic compounds without a vital force; Gupta, 2000); Maxwell's equations for aether. Keating (2002) observed vitalism's "circular reasoning offers pseudo-explanation... we have only labeled our ignorance."

### 5.5.3 Consciousness-as-Fundamental Fits the Pattern

**(a) STEELMAN STATEMENT.** Consciousness-as-fundamental fits the vitalism/aether pattern. It posits a fundamental, unobservable property (consciousness in elementary particles) to explain subjective experience, while emergentist physicalism increasingly accounts for the same phenomena through mechanistic neuroscience: neural correlates of consciousness, predictive processing, global workspace theory, and integrated information measures all generate testable predictions (Doerig et al., 2020; Seth, 2021). Keith Frankish draws the analogy explicitly: consciousness-as-mysterious may follow vitalism's trajectory — once the functions are explained, the "special ingredient" evaporates (Frankish, 2025). Anil Seth makes the same comparison: the hard problem may "dissipate into the ether as vitalism did... by the gradual accumulation of scientific understanding" (Seth, cited in Atlas Writes, 2025). The burden lies on consciousness-as-fundamental to demonstrate why it will not follow the same trajectory.

**(b) PRIMARY SOURCE(S).** Bergson (1911), *Creative Evolution*; Driesch (1914), "The History and Theory of Vitalism"; Wohler (1828), *Annalen der Physik und Chemie*, 88, 253–256; Gupta (2000); Keating (2002); Frankish (2025), podcast, *Prism Global*; Seth (2021), *Being You*; Doerig et al. (2020), *Cognitive Neuroscience*, 11(3), 1–22.

**(c) SEVERITY.** Major. The analogy does not logically entail that consciousness-as-fundamental is false, but it provides strong inductive grounds for skepticism given the uniformly negative track record.

**(d) WHAT WOULD ADDRESS IT.** Demonstrate a disanalogy: show consciousness-as-fundamental has made at least one novel, confirmed prediction vitalism/aether never achieved, and explains something emergentist physicalism *cannot* explain even in principle — not merely something it has not *yet* explained, but something structurally beyond its resources.

**(e) WHAT WOULD NOT.** Claiming vitalism was "different" without specifying the difference (special pleading); appealing to authority of historical figures endorsing consciousness-as-fundamental (they did so without testable predictions — precisely the problem); saying physicalism "hasn't solved consciousness yet" (concedes the comparison is apt, favoring skepticism); arguing vitalism's failure doesn't prove this will fail (true, but absence of disanalogy sustains the inductive concern).

### 5.5.4 Frankish and Seth: The Hard Problem May "Dissipate into the Ether as Vitalism Did"

The most forceful proponents of this analogy are researchers within consciousness studies. Frankish argues the hard problem is conceptual confusion that will resolve as neuroscience progresses, just as "life" resolved into biochemistry (Frankish, 2025). Seth, neither an illusionist nor a physicalist ideologue, makes the vitalism comparison directly. When sympathetic researchers invoke this analogy, the thesis faces an inductive burden that cannot be dismissed as skepticism.

The two Show-stoppers (5.1 and 5.3) are structurally related: Popperian demarcation failure follows from unfalsifiability in principle, since a theory observationally equivalent to its negation generates no potential falsifiers. The three Major ratings reinforce this core diagnosis — Lakatosian degeneration explains *why* the programme has failed to escape, measurement circularity identifies the *mechanism* blocking operationalization, and the vitalism analogy provides *historical precedent*. The manuscript must address all five objections independently — they target different failure modes at different levels of analysis.
