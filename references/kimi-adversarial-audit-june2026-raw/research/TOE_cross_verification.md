## Overlap Analysis

### Expected and Healthy Overlaps

1. **IIT critiques (Domains 3, 4, 5)**: Natural convergence point since IIT is the most developed formal theory. Domain 3 focuses on empirical/testability, Domain 4 on formal/mathematical structure, Domain 5 on methodological status. Complementary rather than redundant.

2. **Unfolding argument (Domains 3, 4, 5)**: Same core problem attacked from three angles — empirical consequences (Domain 3), formal structure (Domain 4), methodological implications (Domain 5). Mutually reinforcing.

3. **Combination problem → Unfalsifiability bridge**: Domain 1's combination problem (CA1) and Domain 5's unfalsifiability (CA3) together create a pincer: either micro-consciousness can't yield macro-consciousness (combination problem), or the whole framework can't be tested (unfalsifiability).

### No Redundancies Requiring Removal

All 25 counter-arguments are distinct in their formulation, primary sources, and angle of attack. The overlaps noted above are cross-reinforcement, not repetition.
