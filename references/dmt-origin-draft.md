### The Origin: An Honest Acknowledgment

I owe you the truth about where this started.

In 2020, I was a lifelong atheist living in a million-dollar home, and I wanted to die. Forty-one years of hardcore materialism had built me a successful career and an empty life. COVID hit. My marriage was crumbling. I sat in that house and seriously considered ending it.

Instead, I tried DMT.

I need to be precise about what happened next, because precision matters in a book that claims to be scientific.

DMT did not prove that consciousness is fundamental. DMT did not give me equations. DMT did not hand me a theory. What DMT did was crack open a door I did not know existed. The experience was — and I will state this plainly because anything less would be dishonest — more real than this reality. Not "felt more real." WAS more real. Billions of geometric corners and not one curved line. A knowing that preceded thought. And a message, simple and devastating: "Everything will be okay."

I came back a different person. Not enlightened. Cracked open.

That crack led to Buddhism. Buddhism led to four months living with monks at Dai Dang Temple in Bonsall, California. The monastery led to a slow rebuild of a shattered worldview. The rebuild led to Baby Viv — a rescued kitten whose death at the jaws of a coyote broke me open in a way DMT never could. And Baby Viv's death led to the question that became this book: does anything in physics give meaning to this?

The chain is: DMT → Buddhism → Monastery → Rebuild → Baby Viv → Question → Framework → Mathematics → Verification.

Every link was necessary. Remove any one and this manuscript does not exist.

#### The Ramanujan Parallel

I am not the first person to receive experiential input that preceded formal mathematics.

Srinivasa Ramanujan attributed his mathematical insights to the Hindu goddess Namagiri, who appeared to him in dreams and presented equations directly. Hardy — the most rigorous mathematician of his era — initially dismissed this as mysticism. Then he checked the equations. They were correct. Many remain unproven to this day, verified only numerically.

I make no claim to Ramanujan's genius. But I recognize the same CATEGORY of event: a direct experiential input, dismissed by the mainstream as subjective or delusional, that preceded and motivated formal mathematical work which subsequently proved independently verifiable.

The DMT experience gave me the conviction that consciousness is fundamental. The double-slit epiphany (September 2025) gave me the mechanism. The AI collaboration gave me the mathematics. The xenon isotope convergence (Li et al. 2018, predicted to 0.3% error) gave me the verification. The genre-locking paper gave me the bridge to established physics.

But without that first crack — without DMT showing me that reality has more layers than materialism admits — I would never have asked the question.

#### Epistemic Honesty

This acknowledgment is Tier 3 — Interpretive. The DMT experience is subjective. It cannot be replicated under controlled conditions in the way that the xenon isotope predictions can. It is not evidence for the framework. It is the origin of the framework.

I state it here because intellectual honesty demands it. A theory's origin does not determine its validity — that is the genetic fallacy. But a theory's origin DOES determine its story. And this story begins with a broken man, a molecule, and a door that should not have been there.

The mathematics stand on their own. The predictions are testable regardless of how I arrived at them. But you deserve to know how I arrived at them.

That is the origin. Now let me show you the science.
