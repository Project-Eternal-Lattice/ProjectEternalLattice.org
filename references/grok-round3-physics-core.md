**1. Wick rotation resolution?**  
No. The original objection is that any real parameter mapping yielding \(P < 2\sqrt{2}\) while preserving a Tsirelson-like interpretation forces \(\tau > 1\), which is unphysical for ordinary quantum correlations. Declaring \(\theta = i\beta\) simply moves the violation off the real line: the resulting \(\tau\) is imaginary and \(P\) becomes complex. Complex correlators are not measurable Bell observables; they do not correspond to any laboratory statistics. Wick rotation is a calculational device that rotates the contour of a path integral; it does not license re-interpreting an algebraic bound violation as “dissipative dynamics on SO(1,1).” The construction therefore replaces one mathematical inconsistency with an equally unphysical analytic continuation. The super-quantum flaw is not resolved; it is hidden behind a change of signature whose physical content remains undefined.

**2. JT dilaton gravity as dynamical theory?**  
It is a kinematic re-packaging. The given action is a 2-D dilaton-gravity model whose equations of motion are satisfied once the metric and connection are chosen to reproduce the algebraic relations \(h(S) = (S^2-4)/4\) and \(A = d\theta - \frac12\sqrt{S^2-4}\,dT\). These relations were already imposed by hand in the original framework; the action merely reproduces them as Euler-Lagrange equations. No new dynamical degree of freedom is introduced that could be varied independently of the original \(\tau\)-\(P\) map. The theory therefore remains a constrained algebraic model dressed in gravitational language, not a predictive dynamical system.

**3. Divergence of \(Z(S)\) at \(S=2\)**  
It is a genuine physical singularity within the stated theory. The scalar kinetic term coefficient \(1/(S^2-4)\) diverges while the dilaton kinetic term acquires a logarithmic singularity. In the JT model the horizon \(S=2\) is where the Killing norm vanishes; here the same locus produces an infinite effective coupling for the phase field \(\theta\). This is not removable by coordinate redefinition (the curvature invariants and the norm of the gradient both diverge). It signals a strong-coupling region that would require a UV completion or an explicit cutoff. Absent either, the classical action is ill-defined precisely at the phase-transition locus the authors wish to interpret as the quantum-to-dissipative boundary.

**4. Harmonic-oscillator geodesics**

The frequency \(\omega = 1/2\) is fixed by the ad-hoc curvature scale of the model and carries no independent empirical anchor. The geodesic therefore predicts unphysical periodic collapse and revival of whatever quantity \(S\) is supposed to represent, with no mechanism for decoherence or measurement back-action.
