# O1 Research: Depth Battery for Consciousness Measurement

## Key Finding 1: Consciousness State Space (CSS) Model
**Source:** Berkovich-Ohana & Glicksohn (2014), Frontiers in Psychology
**URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4010789/

The CSS model proposes THREE dimensions creating a state-space for all phenomenological states:
1. **Time** — subjective temporal experience
2. **Awareness** — phenomenal awareness level
3. **Emotion** — emotional tone/valence

Key structural insight: TWO categories of consciousness:
- **Core Consciousness (CC)** — minimal selfhood, here-and-now, non-conceptual
- **Extended Consciousness (EC)** — narrative selfhood, personal identity, memory, imagination

**Relevance to our τ:** The CSS model's CC maps beautifully to τ→0 (the NOW, the I Am). EC maps to higher τ (multiple competing frames, narrative complexity). The antagonistic dynamics between CC and EC mirror the complementarity structure.

**Testable prediction from CSS:** Flow and meditation REDUCE the antagonism between CC and EC — which in our framework would correspond to approaching the turning point where the trajectory touches the envelope.

## Key Finding 2: 5D-ASC (Five-Dimensional Altered States of Consciousness)
**Source:** Studerus, Gamma & Vollenweider (2010), PLoS ONE (903 citations)
**URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2930851/

The most validated instrument for measuring altered states. Five dimensions:
1. **Oceanic Boundlessness (OB)** — dissolution of ego boundaries, unity experience
2. **Anxious Ego Dissolution (AED)** — loss of control, anxiety
3. **Visionary Restructuralization (VR)** — visual/auditory hallucinations
4. **Auditory Alterations (AA)** — changes in auditory perception
5. **Reduction of Vigilance (RV)** — drowsiness, reduced alertness

**Relevance to σ (saturation ratio):** The 5D-ASC could be a component of the depth battery D. Specifically:
- OB score → correlates with τ (higher OB = deeper consciousness depth)
- The RATIO of OB to AED might approximate σ (saturation efficiency)
- VR could indicate the "fractal phase" at τ≈1

## Synthesis for O1 (Depth Battery D)

A viable depth battery could combine:
1. **5D-ASC** (validated, 903 citations) — measures the phenomenological state
2. **CSS coordinates** (time, awareness, emotion) — provides the state-space location
3. **Neurophenomenological interview** (second-person method) — captures what instruments miss
4. **EEG/fMRI correlates** — provides neural grounding

The σ (saturation ratio) would be:
- σ = (measured 5D-ASC score) / (theoretical maximum for that τ level)
- Where τ level is estimated from the CSS coordinates + neurophenomenological report

**Open question:** How to operationally determine τ INDEPENDENTLY of the battery score, to avoid circularity. The Landau definition (τ² = ¼‖[A₀,A₁]‖·‖[B₀,B₁]‖) gives τ an operator-algebraic meaning, but mapping that to a measurable in a biological system requires identifying what the "observables" A₀, A₁, B₀, B₁ correspond to in neural/cognitive terms.
