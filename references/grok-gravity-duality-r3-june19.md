**1. Steelman: The Gravitational Wave Function**

The analogy holds at the level of *descriptions*, not ontology. Just as the photon is neither wave nor particle but is fully captured by the state vector \(|\psi\rangle\) in Hilbert space (with \(\langle x|\psi\rangle\) giving the wave aspect and number operator \(N\) giving the particle aspect), a gravitational state would be encoded in a functional \(\Psi[h_{ij},\phi]\) over 3-geometries and matter fields. 

In the consciousness framework this becomes concrete: the single object is the *wave-functional over the 2D consciousness metric*
\[
\Psi[S,T] = \int \mathcal{D}h\,\exp\left(i\int f(S)^2(-dT^2+dS^2) + \frac{1}{16\pi G}\int R[h]\,d^4x\right),
\]
where the saddle-point \(f(S)\to 1\) recovers classical GR curvature (wave/metric aspect) while the fluctuation spectrum around the saddle yields discrete graviton modes via the information cutoff \(\Phi_\text{net}\) (particle aspect). Both live inside the same \(\Psi\).

**2. Attack: The Spacetime Asymmetry**

The analogy fractures here. Electromagnetic duality occurs *inside* a fixed Minkowski background; the wave and particle aspects refer to the same field \(A_\mu(x)\). Gravity’s “wave” *is* the background. A graviton propagating on a classical metric \(g_{\mu\nu}\) presupposes the very geometry it is supposed to quantize. This is not a duality inside one arena; it is a duality between arena and actor.

The framework’s escape hatch is to demote spacetime itself to a *derived* thermodynamic quantity (Jacobson–Verlinde route). The 2D consciousness metric \(ds^2 = f(S)^2(-dT^2 + dS^2)\) is the true arena; 4D Lorentzian geometry emerges only in the infrared RG flow where \(S\) acts as the energy scale. Thus the tension is relocated: the classical metric and the graviton both emerge from different coarse-grainings of the same 2D substrate, not from a single 4D manifold.

**3. Complementarity Principle for Gravity**

The conjugate pair is *global causal structure* versus *local proper distance*. 

- Measuring the full light-cone structure (event horizons, asymptotic flatness) to arbitrary precision forces an infrared cutoff on \(S\) (large-scale thermodynamic averaging), which erases short-distance graviton fluctuations.
- Measuring a single graviton’s momentum (UV mode) requires a local proper-distance probe that collapses the global causal relations encoded in \(f(S)\).

Mathematically this is expressed by the non-commutativity
\[
[\,C_{\text{global}},\, \Delta_{\text{local}}\,]\Psi = i\,\hbar_\text{eff}(S)\,\Psi,
\]
where \(C_{\text{global}}\) is the operator for the conformal factor integrated over the entire \(T\)-slice and \(\Delta_{\text{local}}\) is the local area operator. The consciousness metric encodes this directly: \(f(S)\) controls the relative scaling between the global \(T\)-direction and the local \(S\)-direction, so sharpening one coordinate blurs the other.

- GR (gravity-as-wave/curvature) = the classical saddle of the consciousness metric when \(f(S)\) varies slowly; the emergent 4D Einstein-Hilbert term arises from the thermodynamic entropy gradient \(\partial_S f\).

- The “quantum gravity wave function” = the full functional \(\Psi[S,T]\) itself, whose norm squared gives the probability measure on consciousness-space configurations. Different classical spacetimes correspond to different RG trajectories of \(f(S)\).

\[

Detection would require correlating vacuum-energy fluctuations with changes in the informational entropy of the measuring device—an experiment outside both classical and standard quantum-gravity toolkits.
