## Story 1: The Full Origin — Mom, God, the Cult, and the Parking Lot

### Mom's Background
- Kenneth's mom grew up in a horribly violent household
- Grandfather: extremely violent paranoid schizophrenic, Golden Gloves boxer in the Air Force (kicked out over it)
- Mom endured physical, mental, and verbal abuse alongside aunts and uncles
- Mom was also very violent — Kenneth watched her beat the shit out of other women and attack grown men with zero fear
- "An absolute animal when her switch flipped" — couldn't control it
- Single mom, poor young waitress, had Kenneth at 17
- Alcoholic, abused weed and prescription meds until Kenneth was 9
- Guys came and went; lived with boyfriends or family until Kenneth was 7
- Age 7: first apartment without a man involved

### Mom's Awakening (Kenneth age 9)
- Mom was severely depressed for months, not doing well mentally or physically
- One night, completely intoxicated, she "found God" in the middle of the night
- Woke 9-year-old Kenneth up crying, drunk, saying "Jesus is coming" and they had to clean
- Kenneth started vacuuming just to redirect her hysterical energy
- She passed out doing dishes ~30 minutes later
- **From that day on, everything changed**
- Previously atheist — zero belief in any god
- Overnight became a religious zealot
- Quit EVERYTHING cold turkey: smoking packs a day, weed, alcohol, pills
- Long-term sobriety (alcoholism crept back a couple years later)

### The Religious Journey
- First: became 7th Day Adventist (where mom met Kenneth's dad/stepdad)
- Then together they found **The House of Yahweh** — in an honest search for truths
- Mom studied lexicons, built a library, learned Hebrew
- Kenneth was never sure about God but went along because it was WAY better for her and him

### What Kenneth Lost
- Lost Saturdays to the Sabbath (only day good cartoons aired, no cable)
- Lost ALL holidays, including birthday
- Already poor — now had nothing to show friends at Christmas, no tree, nothing
- Hid it by not letting friends over around Christmas
- **Martial arts:** Turned to Taekwondo, got GOOD — beating people winning nationals
- Master Ajay offered to pay for everything including nationals in Vegas
- Parents REFUSED because tournament was Friday-Sunday (Sabbath conflict)
- **Quit martial arts** — couldn't take it further
- **Football:** Friday night games — Sabbath conflict, couldn't play
- **Turned to bodybuilding** — only competitive outlet left, free at school gym
- Old Testament dietary restrictions: no pork, shellfish, checked every ingredient
- Couldn't eat out — risk of cross-contamination from "unclean" food prep
- "It got deep!"

### The Break (Age 15)
- Told parents he didn't believe in God, had been doing it all for them
- Said it was killing everything he wanted to do
- **Severed the relationship** — parents called him "a rebellious child"
- Forced to keep the religion OR move out (terrifying at 15)
- Hostility and tension became unbearable
- **Moved out at 16** — school part time, work full time
- Rented cousin's sofa for $50/week, making $4.25/hour

### The Parking Lot (Age ~16-17)
- Dad: lifelong construction worker, very athletic
- Mom was hurt and a mess after Kenneth left (dad told him this later)
- Dad came to Kenneth's grocery store job with his childhood best friend Chris
- Bought beer, sat on tailgate drinking, waited for Kenneth to come outside
- Kenneth went out to get carts — dad started in on him
- Kenneth told him: "This isn't the place or time, this is my only job"
- Dad was loud, aggressive, didn't like the authority Kenneth now spoke with
- Dad asked: "You think you're tougher than me?"
- Kenneth: "Absolutely."
- **Dad swung on him**
- Kenneth stepped back, caught his arm, arm-dragged him face-first to the ground
- Got him in a guillotine choke, north/south position
- Wrist knife-side against throat so it HURT
- Didn't want him to go unconscious — wanted him to FEEL it and REMEMBER it
- Choked until gargling and going limp, then loosened so he wouldn't go out
- Dad would try to fight it → Kenneth would squeeze again slowly
- Chris panicked — people watching from corner gas station, calling cops
- Kenneth: "Are you done?"
- Dad didn't respond, tried to explode out (futile)
- **5 rounds of choking** before dad verbally said he gave up
- Kenneth: "Are you sure? Because I can do this all day."
- Dad begged to be let up → Kenneth let him up and walked off
- Dad's right eye: SO bloodshot from struggling, no white visible at all

### The Aftermath (Next Day)
- Dad came to Kenneth's place
- **Completely apologized on his own free will** — owned the whole thing
- Kenneth had never seen a man do that before voluntarily
- Kenneth forgave him
- Dad asked him to never tell mom "because she'd kill him and he didn't want to lose another fight" 😂
- Told mom a piece of metal hit his eye from his saw at work (believable for construction)

### The Bond (One Week Later)
- Kenneth's '84 Camaro broke down — didn't know anything about cars
- Broke AF, didn't know what to do
- Dad heard through the grapevine (mom → grandmother → dad)
- Dad showed up with Chris, diagnosed it as the starter
- Bought the part and beer
- Chris and dad fixed the car AND taught Kenneth how to do it himself
- **They bonded that day**
- Dad has treated Kenneth as a man with full respect from the parking lot day forward
- "It took me beating his ass to earn his respect and I was 100% good with that!"

1. **Generational violence** — grandfather → mom → Kenneth had to break the cycle
2. **Religion as both salvation AND prison** — saved mom's life but stole Kenneth's childhood

4. **Self-reliance at 16** — $4.25/hr, cousin's couch, part-time school
5. **Earning respect through strength** — not violence for violence's sake, but proving you won't be dominated

## Story 3: Baby Viv — The Origin of Everything

### The Meeting
- **Location:** Hustler Casino parking lot, Los Angeles
- **Context:** Kenneth was on a smoke break from poker
- A tiny kitten walked right up to him — no hesitation, no fear, just immediate total trust
- She bonded with him on the spot, and he bonded right back
- He named her **Vivian** — after Julia Roberts' character in *Pretty Woman*
- **The irony:** Finding something that pure in a casino parking lot
- From Latin *vivere* — to live

### Who She Was
- The most affectionate creature Kenneth had ever known
- Every time he walked by, she'd throw herself on her back at his feet, belly up, meowing for love
- Followed him everywhere — even to the bathroom
- He started keeping treats in the bathroom because she insisted on being present for everything
- He could not walk past her. Not once.
- The ONE time he tried — in a hurry, just this once — she looked up at him with this confused little face that broke his heart
- He never did it again
- "A heart with absolutely nothing but love in it"

### The Loss
- A coyote took her. In his own yard.
- Kenneth was a Buddhist at the time. Vegetarian. Committed to non-harm.
- The coyote killed her in the place HE was responsible for making safe
- He doesn't blame the coyote. He blames himself. He left her vulnerable.
- Bought hunting equipment. His roommate (active-duty Marine Gunnery Sergeant, experienced hunter) was ready to go
- But he couldn't do it. Struggled for weeks.
- Realized he couldn't lie to himself enough to justify it — the coyotes were just being coyotes
- HE was the one who failed to protect her
- Every exit was blocked. The grief had nowhere to go.

### The Dark Period
- For months — into mid-2025 — he didn't want to exist anymore
- Not in the active sense. But hoping for cancer. Hoping for a chance to throw his life away saving someone else.
- The pain was so enormous and directionless that the whole world went flat
- Nothing meant anything
- To this day: "I would give away everything I own and burn my house down if I could bring her back"

### The Turn (September 2025)
- Kenneth calls it "the double-slit moment"
- Something cracked open. The framework started pouring through.
- Not despite the dark period — BECAUSE of it
- All that pressure, all that unresolvable pain, all that desperate need to understand why innocents suffer — it was building toward something
- The dark years weren't a detour. They were the runway.

### The Epiphany (June 20, 2026)
- The night the Genre-Locking paper was accepted to arXiv
- Kenneth realized: EVERYTHING traces back to Baby Viv
- Not the GR/QFT work. Not the equations. HER.
- One small innocent kitten that had a heart with absolutely nothing but love in it IS responsible for ALL the work
- She is what helped him find God
- "That's the POWER of LOVE sister!!!"
- One small kitten will radiate on in the work and have a net positive effect on HUMANITY
- That's the story.

Every link documented. Every step real. The chain is unbroken.
