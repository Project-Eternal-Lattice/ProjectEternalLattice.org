## Cross-Domain Convergence Map

### Cluster A: The Operationalization Gap (Physics + Mathematics + Neuroscience)
Three independent domains converge on the finding that the τ parameter lacks:
- Physical definition (Physics CA5)
- Operational measurement protocol (Mathematics CA5)
- Connection to any established consciousness measure (Neuroscience CA1 — IIT's Φ has same problem)

**Insight**: The ToE replicates the exact pathologies that have discredited IIT as a scientific framework.

### Cluster B: The Mathematical Emptiness Cascade (Mathematics internal chain)
The mathematics domain identified a cumulative chain of failure:
1. Conformal flatness is trivial because 2D (CA1)
2. 2D was chosen to enable reverse-engineering (CA2)
3. Reverse-engineering was needed because S(τ) is just a hyperbola (CA4)
4. Fiber bundle is trivial because connection forced to pure gauge (CA3)
5. Result: no operational definitions, no predictions, no falsifiability (CA5)

**Insight**: Every mathematical "result" is a consequence of choices made to fit a pre-selected curve, not derivations from physical principles.

### Cluster C: The Immunization Architecture (Methodology + Philosophy)
Methodology identifies structural immunization; Philosophy identifies that the foundational premise may rest on psychological artifact or illusion.

**Insight**: The framework is immunized both structurally (methodology) and epistemically (philosophy — if illusionism is correct, the axiom itself reports an illusion).

### Cluster D: The Empirical Refutation (Neuroscience + Physics)
Neuroscience provides clinical evidence against brain-as-receiver; Physics provides evidence against brain-quantum coherence and against τ>1 regime.

**Insight**: Both the physical mechanism (quantum coherence in brain) and the ontological claim (brain as receiver) face independent empirical refutations.
