## 3. Neuroscience & Information Theory

The Theory of Everything ∞ Law of ONE (ToE) marshals three empirical pillars: (i) Integrated Information Theory (IIT) to quantify consciousness via Φ; (ii) the brain-as-receiver model; and (iii) psychedelic and near-death experiences as evidence that consciousness can access a "source code" layer — the "Traveler." This chapter subjects each pillar to maximum-strength scrutiny. The five counter-arguments that follow draw on primary sources in consciousness neuroscience, clinical neurology, psychopharmacology, and information theory.

---

### 3.1 Integrated Information Theory Has Been Discredited as a Scientific Foundation

**(a) STEELMAN STATEMENT.**

The ToE's use of Φ as a "proto-consciousness measure" — with $S(\tau)$ built atop it — rests on a theory that 124 neuroscientists, philosophers, and cognitive scientists denounced as pseudoscience in a 2023 open letter [^31^]. Reported in *Nature*, the letter argues IIT "masquerades as if it is already very scientifically established" while lacking empirical support [^32^]. Signatories include Dennett, Churchland, Baars, Frankish, and Lau — the core of mainstream cognitive neuroscience [^146^].

The technical objections compound the institutional. Aaronson proved mathematically that IIT assigns unboundedly higher Φ to simple expander graphs (XOR-gate grids) than to human brains, a *reductio* that Tononi himself accepted: "a sufficiently large expander graph would be unboundedly more conscious than humans" [^38^]. Tononi's reply — that this is a "strength" — reveals detachment from scientific constraint [^126^]. Doerig et al.'s (2019) "unfolding argument" proves via the universal approximation theorem that any recurrent network with high Φ can be replicated by a feedforward network with zero Φ, rendering IIT unfalsifiable: no behavioral evidence can distinguish a conscious system from its Φ-zero twin [^27^] [^39^]. The "small network argument" shows IIT assigns consciousness to a single photodiode or thermostat [^36^]. Bayne (2019) argued IIT's "axioms" are not genuine axioms [^37^] [^41^].

The Templeton adversarial collaboration — published in *Nature* 2025 with 256 subjects across 6 labs using fMRI, MEG, and iEEG — found that "a lack of sustained synchronization within the posterior cortex contradicts the claim that network connectivity specifies consciousness" [^132^]. IIT partially failed its own preregistered predictions [^133^]. The ToE's mathematical foundation is therefore built on a theory regarded by mainstream neuroscience as pseudoscientific, facing irreducible technical critiques, and failing direct experimental testing.

**(b) PRIMARY SOURCE(S).**

Lau et al. (2023): 124-scholar letter denouncing IIT as pseudoscience [^31^] [^32^]. Aaronson (2014): expander-graph proof [^38^] [^44^]. Doerig, Schurger, and Herzog (2019): unfolding argument via universal approximation [^27^] [^39^]. Cogitate Consortium et al. (2025): Templeton adversarial collaboration in *Nature* [^132^] [^133^]. Cerullo (2015): computational critique [^36^]. Bayne (2019): axiomatic challenge [^37^] [^41^].

**(c) SEVERITY: Show-stopper.**

The ToE uses IIT's Φ as its "proto-consciousness measure" and constructs $S(\tau)$ as a consciousness depth metric atop this foundation. If IIT is pseudoscientific — as 124 scholars have publicly asserted — the entire mathematical scaffold collapses. The objection targets the framework's central quantitative apparatus.

**(d) WHAT WOULD ADDRESS IT.**

The ToE would need to: (i) demonstrate the 124-scholar letter's arguments are factually wrong; (ii) resolve Aaronson's expander-graph critique mathematically; (iii) solve the unfolding argument's unfalsifiability problem; and (iv) explain why the Templeton collaboration's failure to confirm posterior cortex synchronization does not undermine IIT. Alternatively, it could decouple entirely from IIT and provide an independent mathematical foundation for $S(\tau)$.

**(e) WHAT WOULD NOT.**

Saying "the letter was inflammatory" [^31^] dismisses rather than engages the arguments. Claiming IIT "makes testable predictions" without addressing *which* predictions *failed* is evasion. Appealing to "elegance" as if aesthetics compensate for failed experiments is a category error. Citing Koch's personal commitment substitutes authority for argument. The pseudoscience label must be rebutted substantively, not rhetorically.

### 3.2 The Brain-as-Receiver Model Is Refuted by Clinical Neuroscience

**(a) STEELMAN STATEMENT.**

If the brain merely received consciousness — like a radio receiving a broadcast — then damage would affect reception quality but not abolish specific conscious capacities. This is the opposite of clinical observation. Gage's frontal lobe destruction (1848) produced complete personality transformation — not a "poorer signal" but a different person — with modern neuroimaging confirming bilateral prefrontal damage causing permanent "defect in rational decision making and the processing of emotion" [^73^]. Bilateral PFC damage abolishes specific capacities (emotional experience, future planning) while preserving others [^73^]. Split-brain patients demonstrate *two* independent centers of consciousness — one per hemisphere — each with distinct perceptions and beliefs [^48^]. If the brain were a receiver, splitting it should produce two receivers of the *same* signal, not two different conscious entities. Hemispherectomy permanently removes the conscious capacities of the excised hemisphere; under the receiver model, half a receiver should still receive the same broadcast [^48^]. Lobotomies alter conscious experience in lesion-predictable ways — the pattern predicted by generation, not reception [^158^].

The analogy is also logically incoherent: a radio receives *contents* from an external broadcaster, but *all* contents of consciousness — sensory input, emotions, inner voice, memories — are endogenously generated by the brain [^54^]. "If none of the contents of consciousness are being transmitted from the cosmos into your receiver of a brain, then precisely what is being broadcast apart from all these things?" [^54^]. There is no separate "signal" to receive.

**(b) PRIMARY SOURCE(S).**

Damasio et al. (1994): Gage skull neuroimaging confirming PFC damage correlating with capacity loss [^73^]. Gazzaniga: split-brain research showing two independent conscious centers [^48^]. *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*: lesion pattern (PFC damage alters but doesn't eliminate consciousness; visual cortex damage causes outright blindness) matches generation models [^158^].

**(c) SEVERITY: Show-stopper.**

If the brain generates consciousness, the ToE's Three-Layer Model — with a "Traveler" entering from non-physical Layer 3 — loses its physical foundation. Consciousness becomes emergent from neural organization, not a received signal.

**(d) WHAT WOULD ADDRESS IT.**

The ToE would need to: (i) explain why damage permanently abolishes specific capacities rather than uniformly degrading reception; (ii) account for split-brain duality; (iii) identify what content is broadcast, given all experiential contents are endogenous; (iv) specify the transmission mechanism and broadcast source; and (v) explain why physical neurochemistry modulates consciousness if the brain merely receives it.

**(e) WHAT WOULD NOT.**

"Degraded receiver" metaphors without explaining the specific pattern of permanent loss are hand-waving. Quantum consciousness speculations without empirical evidence do not address clinical findings. Claiming lesion evidence is "compatible with" the receiver model without showing *how* it predicts the patterns observed makes the model unfalsifiable. The receiver analogy cannot be saved by analogical looseness.

### 3.3 Psychedelics Cause Neural Dysregulation, Not "Source-Code Access"

**(a) STEELMAN STATEMENT.**

The ToE claims DMT "breakthrough" accesses "source code" (Layer 3). The leading neuroscientific model, REBUS (Relaxed Beliefs Under Psychedelics), posits that psychedelics increase brain entropy by relaxing precision weighting on high-level priors, liberating bottom-up flow [^114^] [^124^]. This is *disruption* of predictive coding, not access to source code.

Timmermann et al. (2023), in *PNAS* — the most comprehensive DMT neuroimaging study to date (simultaneous EEG-fMRI, 20 volunteers) — found "network disintegration and desegregation," decreased alpha power, increased signal entropy, and "dysregulation of activity in the brain's transmodal association cortex pole" [^119^] [^127^]. The authors describe DMT causing "dysregulation of brain rhythms that would ordinarily be dominant" and a shift "to something altogether more anarchic" [^70^]. This is dysfunction, not privileged access.

The mechanistic problem: DMT is a 5-HT$_{2A}$ receptor agonist — a physical molecule binding to physical receptors, altering neural firing through physical intracellular cascades [^127^]. If the brain received non-physical consciousness, neurochemical modulation should affect reception quality (like adjusting an antenna), not generate entirely new contents. Yet DMT produces novel phenomenology — entities, geometric realms, ego dissolution — because it physically disrupts predictive hierarchy, not because it tunes a receiver to a metaphysical channel.

The ToE's bimodal MEQ30 claim also faces problems: the scale was validated exclusively for psychedelic experiences [^131^], shows no demonstrated bimodal distribution in general populations, and its four-factor structure fits variable degrees of predictive hierarchy disruption, not access to a separate ontological layer [^131^] [^133^].

**(b) PRIMARY SOURCE(S).**

Carhart-Harris and Friston (2019): REBUS model in *Pharmacological Reviews* [^114^] [^124^]. Timmermann et al. (2023): DMT multimodal neuroimaging in *PNAS* [^119^] [^127^]. Barrett, Johnson, and Griffiths (2015): MEQ30 validation for psychedelic contexts [^131^]. MacLean et al. (2012): MEQ30 four-factor structure [^133^].

**(c) SEVERITY: Major.**

The DMT "breakthrough" is a central empirical pillar of the Three-Layer Model. If psychedelic experiences are neural dysregulation rather than access to a non-physical layer, the model's experiential validation collapses. The severity is "Major" only because the ToE could treat Layer 3 as metaphorical — though doing so would evacuate its central ontological claim.

**(d) WHAT WOULD ADDRESS IT.**

The ToE would need to: (i) explain why a 5-HT$_{2A}$ agonist produces "source code access" rather than neural dysregulation; (ii) provide evidence that DMT experiences contain *veridical information* not accessible ordinarily; (iii) demonstrate a genuine bimodal MEQ30 distribution with a sharp threshold at $S(\tau) = 2$; and (iv) explain why anesthetics — which also modulate consciousness physically — do not produce comparable "breakthrough" experiences.

**(e) WHAT WOULD NOT.**

Subjective reports of "other realms" fail, because disrupted predictive hierarchies generate precisely such phenomenology (as REBUS predicts). Anecdotal entity encounters without controlled methodology do not establish veridical content. Claiming REBUS is "compatible with" source-code access without explaining *how* relaxing priors equals metaphysical access is equivocation. Dismissing neural findings as "merely correlates" without addressing why physical receptor agonism should produce non-physical access begs the question.

### 3.4 Competing Theories Explain Consciousness Without Fundamentalism

**(a) STEELMAN STATEMENT.**

The ToE assumes consciousness must be fundamental because "no material process can produce subjective experience." Three well-developed frameworks explain consciousness without this assumption, each with stronger empirical support than IIT.

Graziano's Attention Schema Theory (AST) proposes that consciousness claims derive from the brain's internal model of attention [^47^] [^53^]. Just as the brain constructs a "body schema" to control the body, it constructs an attention schema to control attention — and this simplified model depicts attention as an intangible, non-physical essence [^49^]. AST explains why consciousness *seems* non-physical without requiring it to *be* non-physical. It predicts that disrupting the attention schema alters consciousness claims without affecting attention itself — confirmed experimentally using TMS [^47^].

Dennett and Frankish's illusionism argues that phenomenal consciousness is an introspective illusion [^94^] [^98^]. Frankish (2016): "our sense that it is like something to undergo conscious experiences is due to the fact that we systematically misrepresent them as having phenomenal properties" [^93^]. The brain misrepresents complex neural processes as qualitative properties, just as visual mechanisms produce optical illusions [^95^]. This "solves the hard problem by suggesting how phenomenal consciousness could be illusory" [^99^] — no fundamental substrate required.

Higher-Order Thought (HOT) theories propose a mental state becomes conscious when it is the object of a higher-order representation [^137^] [^158^]. Consciousness is a property of meta-representation, not first-order states. This framework has substantial support from prefrontal cortex studies and makes validated experimental predictions [^157^].

Together, these demonstrate that the hardest problem can be solved without positing consciousness as fundamental. The ToE's core ontological move is unnecessary.

**(b) PRIMARY SOURCE(S).**

Graziano (2019): *Rethinking Consciousness*; AST with TMS confirmation [^47^] [^53^]. Frankish (2016): illusionism in *Journal of Consciousness Studies* [^94^] [^98^]. Dennett (1991): *Consciousness Explained*. Lau and Rosenthal (2011): HOT empirical support in *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* [^157^]. Brown, Lau, and LeDoux (2019): defense of higher-order approach [^137^].

**(c) SEVERITY: Major.**

If alternative, empirically supported frameworks explain consciousness phenomenology without fundamentalism, the ToE's core commitment becomes unnecessary. By parsimony, a framework requiring fewer ontological primitives is preferred. The ToE posits an entire layer (Layer 3) that competitors do without.

**(d) WHAT WOULD ADDRESS IT.**

The ToE would need to: (i) show AST cannot explain some phenomenon the ToE can (e.g., veridical non-attentional information); (ii) demonstrate that illusionism's misrepresentation account fails for specific experiential features; (iii) provide evidence that consciousness exists without higher-order representational capacity; and (iv) argue why consciousness-as-fundamental is more parsimonious despite requiring an additional ontological layer.

**(e) WHAT WOULD NOT.**

Saying functionalist theories "don't explain qualia" without engaging their specific accounts of qualia-claims is question-begging. Introspective certainty cannot rebut illusionism, because illusionism explains that very certainty. Citing the "hard problem" as unsolved by definition ignores the detailed solutions on offer. The ToE must engage these theories at the level of their empirical content.

### 3.5 The Empirical Evidence Cited Does Not Support the Claims

**(a) STEELMAN STATEMENT.**

The ToE selectively interprets ambiguous findings as supportive while ignoring null results and methodological limitations.

**Templeton Collaboration.** The ToE might cite the 2025 *Nature* paper as supporting IIT. In reality, results "substantially challenge key tenets of both theories" [^132^]. For IIT: "a lack of sustained synchronization within the posterior cortex contradicts the claim that network connectivity specifies consciousness" [^132^]. The consortium concluded "neither theory passed all the tests" [^144^]. Selective reading is empirically unjustified.

**Global Consciousness Project.** Independent analyses by NASA astronomer Scargle found apparent deviations "represent chance fluctuations in large datasets, with p-values overstated due to unadjusted multiple testing" [^65^]. Spottiswoode and May found "no statistically significant change in the randomness of the GCP data during the attacks" [^161^]. Bancel (2017) concluded "the data do not support the global consciousness proposal" [^161^]. The project has never been independently replicated and uses post-hoc event selection [^65^] [^74^].

**NDE Research.** AWARE I (Parnia et al., 2014) found only 2% of cardiac arrest patients had OBE-compatible experiences, with one timed auditory case but no hidden image identification [^118^] [^120^]. AWARE II (2023) found 0 of 28 survivors identified hidden visual targets; only 1 of 28 identified the auditory stimulus [^113^] [^116^]. Eleven reported "memories suggestive of consciousness," but these could not be verified as occurring during arrest versus resuscitation or recovery [^81^].

**MEQ30.** The scale was validated exclusively for psychedelic research [^131^] [^133^]. No published study shows a bimodal distribution in general populations at $S=2$. Using a psychedelic-specific instrument to validate a metaphysical model is circular.

**(b) PRIMARY SOURCE(S).**

Cogitate Consortium (2025): *Nature* adversarial collaboration [^132^] [^144^]. Scargle: GCP multiple-comparisons critique [^65^]; Bancel (2017): null conclusion [^161^]. Parnia et al. (2014, 2023): AWARE I and II results [^118^] [^120^] [^113^] [^116^]. Barrett et al. (2015): MEQ30 psychedelic validation [^131^].

**(c) SEVERITY: Major.**

If cited evidence does not support its claims, the framework loses empirical grounding and becomes unfalsifiable — any ambiguous finding can be interpreted post-hoc. The pattern across all three pillars (IIT, psychedelics, anomalous data) is systematic confirmation bias.

**(d) WHAT WOULD ADDRESS IT.**

The ToE would need to: (i) acknowledge Templeton inconclusiveness and explain why this does not undermine Φ-dependent formalism; (ii) provide independent, pre-registered GCP replication with multiple-comparisons correction; (iii) demonstrate veridical NDE information acquired *during* cardiac arrest (not before/after); and (iv) publish peer-reviewed evidence of a bimodal MEQ30 distribution at $S(\tau)=2$ in a non-psychedelic population.

**(e) WHAT WOULD NOT.**

Media headlines about Templeton "favoring IIT" without engaging the *Nature* paper's explicit failure statement is misrepresentation. "More research is needed" as if null results support the theory is *argumentum ad ignorantiam*. Claiming GCP effects are "real but subtle" without addressing multiple comparisons is evasion. Popular NDE books rather than peer-reviewed AWARE results substitute anecdote for evidence. Anecdotal DMT reports without controlled methodology collapse the distinction between phenomenology and evidence.

The table reveals a two-tier challenge. Counter-arguments 3.1 and 3.2 are show-stoppers targeting the mathematical foundation and core ontology respectively. Counter-arguments 3.3–3.5 are "Major" because they undermine empirical validation and philosophical necessity without rendering the framework conceptually impossible. However, these five objections form a cumulative case: the ToE's mathematical foundation is discredited, its ontological model contradicted by clinical evidence, its experiential validation re-interpretable as neural dysfunction, its core philosophical move unnecessary given alternatives, and its evidentiary base systematically over-interpreted. A framework facing show-stopper objections in both formalism and ontology, while its empirical support is challenged across three independent fronts, bears a burden of proof it has not discharged.
