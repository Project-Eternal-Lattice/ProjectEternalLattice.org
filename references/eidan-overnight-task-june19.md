# Eidan's Overnight Task — June 19, 2026

## Context
On the single Bell pair (N=2), the CHSH envelope gives a candidate metric with warp factor f² = 4S²/(S²−4). KEY NEGATIVE RESULT: the S/4 lapse normalization is UNDERIVABLE at N=2 — Jacobson's entanglement equilibrium needs an AREA term, and two qubits have no boundary / no area. The lapse is a CROWD PROPERTY.

## Primary Question
Does an area law emerge in the many-body regime that supplies the missing structure and FIXES the lapse normalization — and if so, at what scale / via what structure?

## Three Lanes

### Lane 1 — LITERATURE MAP
Tiered survey of:
(a) Area laws in many-body entanglement (Eisert-Cramer-Plenio RMP 2010; Hastings; Calabrese-Cardy)
(b) Modular Hamiltonians at finite size + locality (Bisognano-Wichmann; Casini-Huerta; Peschel)
(c) Jacobson entanglement equilibrium in discrete/lattice/tensor-network settings (Jacobson 2016; Cao-Carroll-Michalakis)
(d) Emergent TIME / lapse / metric normalization from many-body entanglement (Lashkari et al; Faulkner et al; Swingle MERA; Connes-Rovelli)

### Lane 2 — CONCRETE COMPUTATION
- Candidate A (GHZ warning case): N-qubit GHZ still Schmidt-rank-2 → likely NO area law → confirm negative
- Candidate B (real one): 1D chain (free-fermion or transverse-field Ising) → compute entanglement entropy, modular Hamiltonian, modular gap + gradient → does area term emerge? Does lapse get fixed?

### Lane 3 — FATE OF THE EDGE
At N=2, S=2 is a curvature singularity. As you tune many-body chain toward product state, does that singular boundary survive, soften, or move?

## Circularity Tripwire
Any "area law fixes the lapse" claim MUST show the area-law coefficient brings GENUINELY INDEPENDENT structure (true geometric area, Newton constant, central charge) — NOT merely another function of the same entanglement spectrum.
