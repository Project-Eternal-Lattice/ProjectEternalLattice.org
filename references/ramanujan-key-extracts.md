## Hardy's Twelve Lectures (1940) — G.H. Hardy
- Title page: "RAMANUJAN: Twelve Lectures on Subjects Suggested by His Life and Work"
- Hardy was Sadleirian Professor of Pure Mathematics, Cambridge
- Published 1940, developed from Harvard Tercentenary Conference lectures (1936)
- Contents: 12 lectures covering primes, round numbers, analytic number theory, lattice-point problems, partitions, hypergeometric series, asymptotic theory of partitions, sums of squares, tau function τ(n), definite integrals, elliptic and modular functions
- Hardy's Preface: "It is not a systematic account of Ramanujan's work (though most of his more important discoveries are mentioned somewhere), but a series of essays suggested by it."
- The book is scanned (image-only PDF, 241 pages)
