## Core analogy
- Reality is compared to a concert.
- The **music** corresponds to how tiny particles are connected to each other (**entanglement**).
- The **dance / crowd motion** corresponds to gravity: how space curves, how things fall, how the universe is shaped.
- Central claim: **different entanglement patterns lead to different gravity theories**.
- Key plain-English sentence: the type of connection between particles **selects** the type of gravity that emerges.
- The field equation is described as a **resonance condition** where the music and dancing reinforce each other.

## The three genres
1. **Area-Law** / standard concert
   - Particles connect mostly to neighbors, not distant particles.
   - Connection scales with **surface area**.
   - Produces **Einstein gravity**.
   - Claimed to emerge naturally rather than being inserted by hand.

2. **Log-Law** / jazz club
   - Appears at transition points between phases.
   - Connections become deeper and scale logarithmically.
   - Produces a flatter, simpler two-dimensional gravity.

3. **Volume-Law** / rave with no pattern
   - Everything connects to everything.
   - Maximum chaos / no coherent geometry.
   - No gravity emerges because there is no structure for it to live in.

## Strong plain-English claims worth integrating
- Einstein's equations "showed up uninvited" when connection strength is constant.
- Calabrese-Cardy also "showed up uninvited," which is framed as stronger evidence because it came from an independent route.
- The TFIM quantum magnet matched the Calabrese-Cardy value to **five significant figures**.
- Two independent implementations by team members produced the same result.
- The solved gravity equation has exactly **one non-trivial solution** with connection strength scaling as **2/3 power of distance**.
- Curvature times the **cube** of connection strength equals a constant.
- The math also yields an inverse-square dilaton potential.

## Newton's constant framing
- The framework's analogue of Newton's gravitational constant is determined by short-distance connection strength.
- Claimed to be computable from particle properties rather than inserted as an unexplained measured constant.
- Claimed stable to 0.01% across system sizes.

## Honest limitations section
- Does **not** explain where the connections come from.
- The **3D+ version is an educated guess** / natural generalization, not first-principles derived.
- Does **not** yet explain matter.
- Time direction is assumed.
- Five-significant-figure result confirms known physics, not a brand-new Level 3 experimental discovery.
- Higher-dimensional theory still faces an observational test and mapping issues.
